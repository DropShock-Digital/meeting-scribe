# Privacy and consent

Meeting Scribe is designed to make the operator's recording decision visible and auditable. It does not decide whether a recording is lawful, sufficient notice has been provided, or every participant has validly consented.

## Defaults
- no automatic recording
- no telemetry
- no default cloud AI or transcription call
- explicit operator disclosure confirmation
- local SQLite/event storage and local exports
- no downstream external write integrations

## Operator responsibilities
Use a clear disclosure appropriate to the people and jurisdiction involved. Restrict the bot and console. Define retention/access/deletion rules before recording sensitive meetings. Obtain required permissions. Test the exact deployment and bot permissions with a non-sensitive meeting first.
